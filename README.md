# Anshu Daswani

Marketer who builds the system, not just the campaign.

Three-time Head of Marketing across five startups, one Fortune 50 rebrand before that. Most recently running marketing at an AI-for-marketers platform, where I build with AI daily: microsites in Lovable with no engineering ticket, content pipelines where agents draft and I edit, and a private operating system that runs my own work.

**What I build here**

[the-ad-standard](https://github.com/anshudaswani/the-ad-standard) is that operating system. Priorities, decisions, capacity, and an interview coach: paste a call transcript and it logs what tested me and what it says about how I interview. Paste a job description and it writes the questions I'm likely to get, with answers in my voice, from a standing bank of the ones I keep getting.

[marketing-skills](https://github.com/anshudaswani/marketing-skills) is the judgment behind it, written down as reusable instructions: how I read an interview, how I prep for one, how I write.
